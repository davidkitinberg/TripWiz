## How to read this document

This document contains the complete cost estimation for the TripWiz production
architecture. It is structured in four parts:

1. **Foundational Assumptions** — every sizing parameter, its rationale, and the source
   of the estimate.
2. **AWS Resource Inventory** — every service in the deployed architecture, confirmed
   directly from `backend/infra/template.yaml` and `frontend/amplify.yml`.
3. **Input Parameters & Derivation** — the exact numbers fed into the AWS Pricing
   Calculator for each service, derived from the assumptions.
4. **Cost Summary** — monthly and annual totals for year 1 (with AWS free tiers) and
   steady-state (after free tiers expire), with cost-driver analysis.

> at the following shareable link and was exported as a PDF for submission:

## Part 1 — Foundational Assumptions (הנחות יסוד)

Before calculating anything, we established realistic sizing parameters for a B2C
travel-planning SaaS in its first year of operation. These figures are intentionally
conservative so the estimate represents a credible baseline rather than a best-case
scenario.

### 1.1 User Base

| Parameter | Estimate | Rationale |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Registered users at launch (month 1) | **1,000** | Early-adopter + initial marketing campaigns |
| Registered users at year-end | **35,000** | Midpoint of 20K–50K growth target |
| Monthly Active Users (MAU) | **7,000** | 20% of 35K — travel apps are inherently seasonal and sporadic |
| Peak concurrent users (CCU) | **350** | 5% of MAU — weekend evenings or pre-summer surge |
| Month-over-month growth rate | **17.5%** | Midpoint of healthy 15–20% MoM target |

### 1.2 Usage Patterns (per active user per month)

| Parameter | Estimate | Rationale |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Trips created per active user/month | **1.5** | Tourists travel 1–3×/year; active users create 1–2 trips |
| Itinerary stops per trip | **17** | Typical 3–5 day itinerary: hotels, attractions, restaurants, flights |
| AI Optimize calls per trip | **4** | Iterative trial-and-error until the user is satisfied with the route |
| Weather Validate calls per trip | **2.5** | Usually triggered near departure or after stop changes |
| Place searches per planning session | **20** | Searching for points of interest is the most frequent in-app action |
| Live co-edit WebSocket sessions per trip | **2** | Collaborative planning between travel partners |
| Average WebSocket session duration | **22 min** | Midpoint of 15–30 min observed range |

### 1.3 Storage (per user / per trip)

| Parameter | Estimate | Rationale |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| File attachments uploaded per trip (S3) | **4 files** | Flight tickets, hotel vouchers, attraction passes |
| Average file size | **1.75 MB** | PDFs and images — midpoint of 1.5–2 MB range |
| DynamoDB trip item size | **20 KB** | JSON with 17 stops, metadata, permissions, S3 keys (no binary content) |

### 1.4 Notifications & Scheduling

| Parameter | Estimate | Rationale |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Trip reminder opt-in rate | **75%** | High opt-in expected for schedule-tied travel notifications |
| Weather alert SNS messages per day | **500** | ~5% of 10K active-traveller-day users have active trips on any given day |

## Part 3 — Input Parameters & Derivation per Service

### Lambda (7 functions)

| Function | Invocations/month | Avg duration | Memory | GB-seconds |
|----------|------------------|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| `TripWiz-Trips` | 700,000 | 500 ms | 512 MB | 179,200 |
| `TripWiz-WebSocketHandler` | 1,050,000 | 100 ms | 128 MB | 13,440 |
| `TripWiz-WebSocketAuthorizer` | 42,000 | 200 ms | 128 MB | 1,075 |
| `TripWiz-Validate` | 79,000 | 2,500 ms | 256 MB | 50,400 |
| `TripWiz-TripReminder` | 30 | 30,000 ms | 256 MB | 230 |
| `TripWiz-SnsToSes` | 15,000 | 200 ms | 128 MB | 384 |
| `TripWiz-PostConfirmation` | 500 | 150 ms | 128 MB | 9 |
| **Total** | **1,886,530** | | | **244,738** |

**`TripWiz-PostConfirmation` derivation (500/mo):** ~500 new sign-up confirmations per
month against the 7,000 MAU base; single conditional `PutItem` to DynamoDB, ~150 ms.

**REST invocations derivation (700K):** 7,000 MAU × ~100 API calls/user/month
(list trips ×30, stop operations ×25, place searches ×30, AI/validate/misc ×15).

**Validate invocations (79K):** 26,250 on-demand (7K × 1.5 × 2.5) + 52,500 scheduled
(1,750 upcoming trips/day × 30 days).

Permanent free tier: 1M requests/month + 400,000 GB-s/month.
244,729 GB-s < 400,000 → **Lambda GB-second cost = $0**.
Only 886K requests over free tier are billable.

### Cost-Driver Analysis

```
Amazon Bedrock (AI route optimisation):  $173.88/month  →  55.5% of calculator total
                                                         →  87.7% of year-1 cost

Amazon Location Service:                 $110.25/month  →  35.2% of calculator total
                                         $   0.00/month  →  year 1 (12-month free tier)

Everything else combined:                $ 28.01 /month  →   8.9% of calculator total
                                         $ 24.43 /month  →  12.3% of year-1 cost
```

**Key observations:**

1. **Bedrock is the dominant and constant cost.** At $173.88/month it accounts for
   over half the total bill in all scenarios and is unaffected by any free tier.
   It scales linearly with AI optimize usage. Reducing the per-trip call limit from
   4 to 3 saves approximately $43/month (~25%).

2. **Amazon Location Service creates a sharp year-2 pricing cliff.** The 12-month
   free tier covers our 210K monthly searches with 93% headroom to spare, making
   year 1 essentially free for this service. After expiry it jumps to $110.25/month
   — the second-largest line item. A client-side geocoding cache deduplicating
   repeated searches within a session can reduce billable requests by an estimated
   30–40%, lowering this to ~$65–$80/month.

3. **The serverless architecture delivers on its cost promise.** Lambda costs $0.01,
   DynamoDB $4.75, API Gateway $3.50, and CloudWatch $0.00 — all six backend compute
   services combined are under $10/month. The platform handles demand spikes without
   provisioned capacity changes.

4. **Cognito is permanently free at this scale.** At 7,000 MAU we are 14% of the
   50,000 MAU free threshold. It only becomes a line item if TripWiz grows beyond
   ~55,000 monthly active users.

### Monthly Cost Projection (Year 1 growth curve)

Bedrock cost scales proportionally with MAU ($173.88 ÷ 7,000 MAU = ~$24.84 per
1,000 MAU/month). All other services are within free tiers or near-fixed through
most of year 1.

| Month | Registered Users | MAU | Bedrock | Other services | Est. Monthly Cost |
|------:|----------------:|----:|--------:|---------------:|------------------:|
| 1 | 1,000 | 200 | ~$5 | ~$10 | **~$15** |
| 3 | 1,372 | 470 | ~$12 | ~$12 | **~$24** |
| 6 | 2,436 | 1,000 | ~$25 | ~$15 | **~$40** |
| 9 | 4,321 | 3,100 | ~$77 | ~$20 | **~$97** |
| 12 | 7,662 | 7,000 | ~$174 | ~$24 | **~$198** |

The ramp is gentle in months 1–6 because Bedrock dominates and MAU is still low.
The 12-month free-tier savings on Location Service and REST API Gateway keep
non-AI costs near-zero for the entire first year.
